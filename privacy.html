Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction
Well Prime Insurance LLC respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we collect, how we use and protect that information, and how information related to our SMS messaging program is handled.

2. Information We Collect
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you visit our website, submit an insurance inquiry, request an insurance quote, request customer service assistance, schedule an appointment, or otherwise communicate with Well Prime Insurance LLC.

Information collected may include your name, email address, mobile or telephone number, contact information, and information reasonably necessary to respond to your insurance inquiry or provide the services you request.

3. How We Use Your Information
Well Prime Insurance LLC may use the information you provide to:
Respond to insurance inquiries.
Assist with requested insurance quotes.
Provide customer service.
Follow up regarding insurance requests.
Provide application or appointment updates.
Send reminders related to services you requested.
Communicate information necessary to assist with your insurance inquiry.
Maintain and improve our services and communications.

4. SMS Communications
Customers and prospective customers may voluntarily opt in to receive SMS messages from Well Prime Insurance LLC.

If you opt in, messages may include communications related to insurance inquiries, requested quotes, customer-service requests, application or appointment updates, reminders, and related follow-up communications.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from SMS communications. Reply HELP for assistance.

Participation in our SMS program is voluntary. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing or receiving any goods or services from Well Prime Insurance LLC.

5. SMS Opt-In and Consent
When SMS consent is collected through our website, users provide their mobile telephone number and affirmatively select a separate SMS consent checkbox.

The SMS consent checkbox is not pre-selected, and users may submit their primary inquiry or request without consenting to SMS communications.

6. SMS Privacy and Non-Sharing
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Mobile phone numbers, text messaging originator opt-in data, and SMS consent collected by Well Prime Insurance LLC will not be sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

This restriction applies specifically to SMS consent and mobile opt-in information and is intended to preserve the direct consent relationship between the consumer and Well Prime Insurance LLC.

7. Sharing of Other Personal Information
Well Prime Insurance LLC does not sell personal information.

We may disclose personal information when reasonably necessary to:
Provide services requested by you.
Work with service providers that perform operational services on our behalf.
Comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or legal obligations.
Prevent fraud or misuse.
Protect the rights, safety, security, or property of Well Prime Insurance LLC, our customers, or others.

However, SMS opt-in data, mobile telephone numbers collected for SMS enrollment, and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

8. Data Security
We use reasonable administrative, organizational, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, or destruction.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information entrusted to us.

9. Data Retention
We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, maintain business records, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

10. Your Communication Choices
You may choose whether to receive SMS communications from Well Prime Insurance LLC.

To stop receiving SMS messages, reply STOP to any SMS message from us. After opting out, you may receive a confirmation message acknowledging your request.

For assistance, reply HELP or contact us using the information listed below.

11. Third-Party Service Providers
We may use service providers to support website hosting, communications, information technology, security, analytics, or other operational functions.

Service providers are permitted to process information only as appropriate for the services they provide to Well Prime Insurance LLC and subject to applicable requirements.

SMS opt-in consent will not be transferred to third parties for their own marketing or promotional use.

12. External Websites
Our website may contain links to third-party websites. Well Prime Insurance LLC is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites operated by third parties.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy
Well Prime Insurance LLC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or services.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with the applicable effective date.

14. Contact Us
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, our privacy practices, or our SMS messaging program, contact:
Well Prime Insurance LLC
Email: support@wellprimeinsurance.com

For additional information about our SMS program, please review our SMS Terms & Conditions.
