About Well Prime Insurance LLC

About Our Company
Well Prime Insurance LLC is an insurance services and professional training company based in Smithfield, Rhode Island. We provide insurance-related assistance to consumers and practical education and coaching services to insurance professionals and independent agencies.

Our Insurance Services
Our insurance services focus on helping consumers with insurance inquiries, requested quotes, coverage options, customer-service needs, application and appointment follow-ups, and other insurance-related requests. Our team works with customers to understand their needs and assist them with available insurance solutions and next steps.

Agency MasterClass
In addition to consumer insurance services, Well Prime Insurance LLC operates Agency MasterClass, a professional education and coaching program designed for Property & Casualty insurance agents, aspiring insurance professionals, and independent agency owners.

Training and Practical Resources
Agency MasterClass provides training and practical resources in areas including:
Insurance sales and customer communication
Lead generation and lead conversion
Customer retention and referrals
Quoting and sales workflows
Objection handling and ethical closing techniques
Trucking and commercial insurance sales strategies
Agency workflow and productivity
Client onboarding and follow-up
Sales scripts, email templates, and text-message templates
Live coaching and on-demand training

Our Goal
Our goal is to help consumers receive responsive insurance assistance while giving insurance professionals practical tools, education, and coaching that can help them improve customer service, strengthen sales skills, and grow their agencies.

Customer Communications
Customers and prospective customers may contact Well Prime Insurance LLC through our website, telephone, email, or other available communication channels. Customers who separately and voluntarily opt in to SMS communications may receive text messages regarding their insurance inquiry, requested quote, customer-service request, application or appointment updates, reminders, and related service follow-ups.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.
